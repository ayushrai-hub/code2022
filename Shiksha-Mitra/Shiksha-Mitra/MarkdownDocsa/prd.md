# Project Requirement Document (PRD) – Shiksha-Mitra

## 1. Introduction

This PRD outlines the requirements for **Shiksha-Mitra**, an AI-powered educational platform designed to bridge the gap between **students, teachers, and learning resources**.  
The platform will provide **personalized support, dynamic learning paths, and collaboration features**, creating a holistic ecosystem for education.

---

## 2. Project Goals & Objectives

**Primary Goal:** Empower learners and educators with a next-generation, AI-driven education companion.  

### Key Objectives:
- Enable personalized learning through AI-driven recommendations.  
- Connect students with teachers, mentors, and peers in real time.  
- Provide a structured platform for content delivery, doubt resolution, and assessment.  
- Enhance accessibility for learners across rural and urban areas.  
- Foster collaboration and community-led knowledge sharing.  

---

## 3. Target Audience

- **Students:** School, college, and self-learners seeking guidance.  
- **Teachers & Mentors:** Professionals who want to share knowledge and earn through tutoring.  
- **Parents:** Looking for performance tracking and reliable resources for their children.  
- **Institutions:** Schools, colleges, and coaching centers seeking digital transformation.  

---

## 4. Key Features & Functionality

### 4.1. Core Modules
- **Onboarding & Profiles:** Separate flows for students, teachers, and institutions.  
- **AI Learning Assistant:** Personalized study plans, Q&A chatbot, and adaptive quizzes.  
- **Courses & Content:** Structured lessons, video lectures, and interactive material.  
- **Doubt Clearing:** Real-time chat, video call, or asynchronous Q&A threads.  
- **Assessment & Progress Tracking:** Quizzes, assignments, report cards, and learning analytics.  
- **Community Hub:** Discussion forums, peer learning groups, and collaborative projects.  
- **Teacher Dashboard:** Class scheduling, student engagement metrics, and earnings overview.  
- **Student Dashboard:** Personalized timeline, progress charts, and reminders.  
- **Gamification:** Badges, leaderboards, and reward points to boost engagement.  
- **Multilingual Support:** Regional language availability for inclusivity.  

### 4.2. Visual & Technical Requirements
- **Theme:** Clean, educational, and trust-building design.  
- **Color Palette:** Sky blue, deep green, white, and light gray with accent gradients.  
- **Typography:** Headers (Poppins/Roboto), Body (System UI / Lato).  
- **Animations:** Subtle micro-interactions, progress bars, and onboarding flows.  
- **Responsiveness:** Mobile-first approach for accessibility across devices.  
- **Performance:** Optimized loading, caching strategies, and lightweight design.  
- **Accessibility:** Screen reader support, ARIA compliance, and high-contrast mode.  

### 4.3. Back-end & Integrations
- **Authentication:** Secure login (OAuth, Google, Phone OTP).  
- **Database:** PostgreSQL/MongoDB for users, courses, and analytics.  
- **Video & Chat:** WebRTC, Zoom SDK, or Twilio for real-time classes.  
- **Payments:** Razorpay/Stripe integration for subscriptions and tutoring fees.  
- **AI/ML Layer:** Recommendation engine, chatbot, and adaptive assessments.  
- **CMS:** For managing content, blogs, and institutional updates.  
- **Analytics:** Google Analytics + custom dashboards for insights.  

---

## 5. Development & Deployment

- **Front-end:** Next.js or React with TypeScript.  
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS for scalable UI design.  
- **Back-end:** Node.js/Express or Django for APIs.  
- **Hosting:** Vercel/Netlify (front-end), AWS/GCP/Azure (back-end).  
- **Version Control:** Git with GitHub for collaboration.  
- **CI/CD:** Automated deployment pipelines.  
- **Domain:** Custom domain with SSL for trust and security.  

---

## 6. Maintenance & Future Scope

- Continuous integration of new learning modules and AI upgrades.  
- Expansion into AR/VR classrooms and gamified simulations.  
- Partnerships with institutions for verified courses.  
- Mobile apps (Android/iOS) for broader adoption.  
- Regular security audits and performance tuning.
